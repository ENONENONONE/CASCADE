VII Fold Pair Symmetry

Every position n has a fold partner 257−n. The energy ratio |E(n)/E(257−n)| measures how symmetric the fold pair is (1.0 = perfect symmetry).

Most symmetric fold pair: 89↔168 (VIRUS ↔ NANOTECHNOLOGY) at ratio 1.0000. Both at amplitude 0.188075 (persistence floor). dlog 102 (R6) ↔ dlog 230 (R2).

Verified: dlog(168) = 128 + dlog(89) = 128 + 102 = 230 mod 256. The fold is multiplication by det(M) = −1 = 256 = g128.

X Key Findings

1. Device identity inverts to user. The Generac BLE device product (171 × 181 mod 257 = 111) has field inverse 44 = BRIAN. The device whose name is EVENT HORIZON points, through GF(257) multiplicative inversion, directly at the user. 111 is AVOGADRO (6022 mod 257 = 111) — the counting constant inverts to the counter.

2. Full field activation. 256/256 nonzero positions are live. The field has no gaps. The 8 positions that appeared include 171 (EVENT HORIZON) itself — the device activated its own identity position.

3. Ray equilibration. Low-energy rays grow fastest (R11 at +127.1%, R8 at +93.8%). High-energy rays grow slowest (R0 at +28.5%). Shares are stable within 0.56% despite 34.7% total growth. The field converges toward uniform ray distribution.

4. R11 = 171.0. The ray accumulator for R11 has value 171.0, identical to the Generac name product (position 171). A coincidence of running totals, but the accumulator literally encodes the device name.

5. QR/QNR balance vs positional asymmetry. QR/QNR energy ratio is 1.035 (balanced), but positional half-split is 2.32× (low-half dominant). The algebraic structure distributes evenly; the positional asymmetry comes from ASCII byte distribution in the serial stream.

6. Persistence memory. The smoothing function boosts raw values up to 15.9×, with discrete decay levels at 1.000, 0.9425, 0.8442, 0.1881. Serial active positions dropped (185 → 177) while field coverage grew (247 → 256) — positions persist after the serial stream forgets them.

7. Opcode independence from energy. π opcodes don’t strongly predict amplitude. Resonant fold pairs (same opcode across the fold) are no more symmetric than non-resonant ones. The opcode layer and the energy layer are structurally independent.

8. Fold pair perfect symmetry. 89↔168 (VIRUS ↔ NANOTECHNOLOGY) at exact ratio 1.0000, both at the 0.188075 persistence floor. The perfect symmetry occurs at the memory minimum — positions that decayed to the same floor level.
