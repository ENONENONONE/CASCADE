The Single Axiom

The axiom is not chosen for φ; it is chosen for the conjunction of symmetry and order, and φ is what falls out. No other irrational satisfies the constraints that follow.

Spoken vs Internal Speech

HYPOTHESIS REJECTED: same language, NOT same circuit. Internal and spoken speech show different neural patterns.
  • Internal monologue is MORE active: 8–11% higher phase variance
  • Spoken speech shifts energy to exit zone (sc 38–63) — skull vibration from phonation
  • Internal speech has higher cognitive load: BR 39.7 vs 24.0 (65% higher)
The internal voice is not “pre-speech.” Higher degrees of freedom, higher cognitive load, different spatial pattern. Internal monologue is its own mode.

Every layer of BWave maps through the same field arithmetic used across the entire framework.

The Connection

The IQ data format is 64 [I,Q] pairs — complex values in the field. The phase angle of each pair IS a field element’s argument in GF(257²), the extension field where φ lives. The same modular exponentiation, dlog tables, and ray mapping used in the emerald tablet, the cascade machine, and the carrier map are used here on live RF data from human tissue.

The GF(257) structure is not applied to the data. It is how the OFDM subcarriers were already organized by the WiFi standard.
