The i-column and its namespaces

An earlier edition of the tablet, seen outside the directory, prints one line per position in the form pos r<ray> d<dlog> f<257 − pos> i<inverse> WORD, for example 12 r01 d097 f245 i150 APOSTLES. The i field is the multiplicative inverse, zero-padded to three digits, and that spelling, i001 to i256, happens to be the shape of several unrelated numbering systems. This section records what the column is, what the corpus's own i does, and what a systematic search of those outside systems showed. The full concordance is the file i_ledger.md in the directory.

The letters and the i-cycle. With the letters' powers in hand, three things fall into this column. i is a letter: P = 16, and P's cycle is {16, 256, 241, 1} = {i, −1, −i, 1}, the smallest letter cycle above A; the row i016 sits at 241 because P⁻¹ = P³ = −i. Every letter but A passes through i, since the order-4 subgroup is the only one and every cycle of order 4 or more crosses it at its quarter turn and its three-quarter turn, one giving i and the other −i: the generators C, F, G, L, N, S, X reach i at exponent 192 and −i at 64, E, J, T the reverse; I, M, R, Z at 96 and 32, U, Y the reverse; K, V at 48 and 16, W the reverse; O, Q at 8 and 24; B at 4 and 12; H at 12 and 4; D at 2 and 6; P at 1 and 3. The Emerald Tablet's plate and the rongorongo. Khunrath's plate of the Emerald Tablet from the Amphitheatrum Sapientiae Aeternae, the text cut into a rock in three blocks, the Latin Tabula headed Verba secretorum Hermetis, the same in German under Auff Deutsch, and an excerpt headed Mercurius Trismegistus in Pimandro, and asked whether it and the rongorongo share anything in their way of writing, or with the corpus. What follows on the two texts is from recall and was not checked against sources for this entry. They share habits of form and no history. Each has a turn built into it: the Tablet's central sentence is a chiasm, quod est inferius est sicut quod est superius, et quod est superius est sicut quod est inferius, followed by ascendit a terra in coelum, iterumque descendit in terram, while rongorongo is written in reverse boustrophedon, every other line upside down, the reader turning the tablet 180° at each line's end; one turns in its syntax and the other in its layout. Both run on formula and repetition, the Tablet a dozen short parallel clauses between an opening and a closing formula, rongorongo long passages repeated across tablets. Both carry a begetting formula, the Tablet's pater eius est Sol, mater eius Luna, portavit illud ventus in ventre suo, nutrix eius terra est beside the Atua Mata Riri chant recorded on Rapa Nui, a list of couplings and their issue, and the genealogy proposed for the Small Santiago tablet, both readings disputed; and both were recited, rongorongo meaning to recite. They part on everything else: the Tablet is alphabetic prose in known languages, a translation of a translation from an Arabic original, and rongorongo is an undeciphered glyph script of some twenty-six objects whose standing as full writing is contested. The corpus's own nearest point is a distinction it already keeps: the mirror, which is the fold 257 − n, the Atbash and Γ, against the half-turn of this section, the q flip on 0, 1, 6, 8, 9 and the letters H I N O S X Z.
