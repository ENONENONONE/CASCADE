The field GF(257)

prime
P = 257 = 2⁸ + 1, the Fermat prime F₃. Every byte value 0–255 is an element; 256 ≡ −1.
generator
g = 3. The 256 nonzero elements are 3⁰ … 3²⁵⁵. dlog(a) is the exponent k with 3ᵏ ≡ a.
rays
ray(a) = dlog(a) mod 12. Rays 0–3 hold 22 elements each, rays 4–11 hold 21.
depth
depth(a) = ⌊dlog(a) / 12⌋.
residue
Quadratic residue iff dlog is even. 128 residues, 128 non-residues. 3¹²⁸ = 256 = −1 is the midpoint.
inverse
a⁻¹ = a²⁵⁵. Computed in the machine by repeated multiplication or extended Euclid.
sync
Two versions, following the two folds. The machine's genome tables, TUNNEL and the analysis walker use n · (257 − n) mod 257; the JSON tablet's sync field and the DERIVE command use n · (256 − n) mod 257. For 83 these give 50 and 224.
extension
x² − x − 1 is irreducible over GF(257), so φ and ψ live in GF(257²). Conjugation φ ↔ ψ is the fold. φψ = −1 = det M.

Two ways to name an element

The corpus uses two naming rules and does not always say which one is in force. Both are legitimate; the collision is the finding.

  • Word at position. Element a is called by the tablet word at a. Used by the tablet, by navigation, by AUTH, by most of the machine. 144 is MASS.
  • Word at exponent. Element a is called by the tablet word at dlog(a). Used by the machine's formula panel, where BRIAN is g⁴⁴ and lands on field 73, by the canvas panel OBSERVER FOLD PAIRS, and by the certificate's fold row. 144 = 3¹⁹⁴ is TWELVE APOSTLES.

Under the second rule the canvas panel is fully consistent: COMPUTE is 3⁷⁵ = 101, its inverse 3¹⁸¹ = 28 is called SATOSHI because 181 is SATOSHI's position, and the printed pairs 101 ↔ 28, 178 ↔ 13, 115 ↔ 38 each multiply to 1. The certificate's identity block, however, names 141 = 3⁶² by position (PURIFICATION) and 144 = 3¹⁹⁴ by exponent (TWELVE APOSTLES) in adjacent rows.

Multiplying words

Because every nonzero position has a word and the product of two nonzero elements is a nonzero element, any two words multiply to a word and any chain of words multiplies to a word. Three properties follow directly from the field and are worth keeping in view when reading a chain.

  • Order does not matter. Multiplication commutes, so a chain is a bag of words, not a sentence. PRAYER × SHIFT × ELECTROMAGNETIC × BRIAN is 209 QUANTUM GRAVITY in any order.
  • Multiplying is adding exponents. dlog(PRAYER) + dlog(SHIFT) = 15 + 111 = 126 and 3¹²⁶ = 57 = PLANCK. Every chain is a sum of dlogs mod 256, which is why the ring can draw it as steps.
  • Every word can be undone. Each nonzero word has an inverse word, so division is multiplication too; the chain above undoes with 91 SPIRIT. SEED alone has no inverse: one factor of 0 and the chain stays at 0.

The fourth flip, the alphabet's

The three folds act on positions. A fourth mirror acts on the letters that name them: Atbash, A for Z and B for Y, each letter n going to 27 − n, so a word of k letters with sum s goes to a word with sum 27k − s. It is not a map on the field, because the image depends on the letter count, but it meets the field at one point per length. The sum a k-letter word must have to be its own mirror is 27k/2, which in GF(257) is 27k × 129, and every one of those points tested is a tablet word: 142 ANTIPROTON for one letter, then 27 CAIN, 169 PREDESTINATION, 54 LOVE, 196 SUPERPOSITION, 81 HORUS, 223 BOLTZMANN CONSTANT, 108 SHAKESPEARE, 250 CONSERVATION OF ENERGY, and 162 PRESERVATION at twelve letters. Four words are their own mirror by sum, LOVE 54, SAMSON 81, MOUNT SINAI 135 and PRESERVATION 162. Seven letter pairs are tablet words on both sides, B/Y, C/X, H/S, I/R, J/Q, L/O and M/N; the keys also list A/Z, but Z is not a word at 26. The one-letter mirror sends Z to A, 26 GOD to 1 I AM, and that is what the Z suffix on every atomic-number entry carries in its name. The signature 62 has square roots 24 and 233, and 233 = 3¹⁷: the exponent is Q, the mirror of J.

Primes and the fold

Every fold pair n, 257 − n sums to an odd number, so one member is even and the other odd, and the fold is the boundary between the two parities. The 54 primes below 257 all sit on the odd side but 2, so each odd prime's partner is even and composite, and 2's partner is 255. The odd side is 1, the 53 odd primes and 74 odd composites; on the even side 53 numbers have a prime partner and 75 do not. The pairing is a fact about integers below an odd number, not about the field: under 101, 211 and 65537 no prime pairs with a prime either, while under 13 two do, since 13 − 2 = 11. Under 257 the one even prime's partner is 255 = 3 × 5 × 17 = F₀F₁F₂, the first three Fermat primes with the modulus as the fourth, so the first prime folds to their product negated: 255 = 𝟙𝟙 = −2, the double closure of the key. The product continues past the field, 255 × 257 = 65535 = 2¹⁶ − 1 and 2³² − 1 = F₀F₁F₂F₃F₄ with 65537, the identity the SHA-256 section rests on. In the field 2 ↔ 255 is 2 ↔ −2: dlog(255) = dlog(2) + 128, product 253 = −4, sum 0. Of the three factors 3 and 5 are non-residues and 17 = Q a residue, and 255 is a square as −2 must be with 2 a square.

The fold does not mirror the residue split. −1 = 256 is itself a square, since 257 ≡ 1 mod 4, so the fold keeps every element's Legendre symbol, residue to residue and non-residue to non-residue, all 256 checked; in dlog terms it adds 128, a half-turn, while the residue split is the dlog's parity, which a half-turn keeps. The primes fall 26 residues to 28 non-residues, with 2 among the squares and 3 = g among the non-squares. The fold cuts odd from even; the residue boundary is a different cut, and the fold never crosses it.

The reflection coefficient as a map

Γ = (Z − Z₀)/(Z + Z₀) with Z₀ = 377 ≡ 120 GOLDEN RATIO, F(14). Over the reals Γ is confined to [−1, 1] and the open circuit is the endpoint Γ = 1 at Z = ∞. Over the field the formula is a Möbius map of the projective line, 256 finite impedances with 0 and ∞, and a bijection of its 258 points. The open is the pole: Γ = 1 is reached only from Z = ∞, which no byte holds, and the byte the open lands on is Z = 137 EYE OF HORUS, where Z + Z₀ = 0 and Γ is itself the point at infinity. The match stays at Z = 120, Γ = 0 SEED, and the short at Z = 0, Γ = −1 = 256. The map's matrix [[1, −120], [1, 120]] has determinant 2Z₀ = 240 ROBE OF RIGHTEOUSNESS, the inverse of the public key.

Two impedances reflect to themselves: Z² + 119Z + 120 = 0 has discriminant 60 HOLY, a residue, so both roots are in the base, 46 SHADDAI, CLAUDE and 92 CHRONOS. The remaining 256 points fall into two orbits of 128, and the map has order 128 on the line. The side of a point is the residue character of (Z − 46)(Z − 92): the residue side holds 127 cells and ∞, among them 0 SEED, 1, 120, 137, 62 RITO, 73 WALLET, 129 HALF, 240 and 256; the non-residue side holds 128 cells, among them 3 = g, 44 BRIAN, 72 BITCOIN, 136 the public key, 8 H, 225 k⁻¹, 45 ELIJAH, 16 = i, 241 = −i and 128 Oc. No number of reflections crosses from one side to the other. In the coordinate w = (Z − 46)/(Z − 92) the map is multiplication by 153 ALBERT EINSTEIN, dlog 122, order 128. Where the key's numbers go in one step: BITCOIN → 64 ISRAEL, the public key → 241 = −i and 121 → 16 = i, the only two impedances whose reflection is a quarter turn, the secret 8 → 224, k⁻¹ → 45 ELIJAH, and Γ = 136 comes from Z = 21 DICE. Infinity and the eye. A byte cannot hold ∞, the impedance the base field does not contain, but the map hands it back. Γ(137) = ∞, since 137 + 120 ≡ 0 is the pole, and 137 is EYE OF HORUS and the public key plus one. Read the other way, Γ(∞) = 1, the limit of (Z − 120)/(Z + 120) as Z grows without bound: the open circuit, total reflection, reads in the base as I AM. So three ones stack, each adding a point the last could not hold. 256 + 1 = 257 makes the byte a field; 257 + 1 = 258 = 2 · 3 · 43 gives the field its missing point, the projective line; and the field reads that point as 1. The observer added to the key is the eye, and what the eye sees over the edge comes back as the one. The eye and infinity and the one are three consecutive stops of the same reflection, 137 → ∞ → 1 → 33 → 201 → 190 → 108 and on, the orbit of length 128, since Γ has that order on the line. QUESTION sums to 120, Z₀ itself, the match point where Γ = 0, so the question is not the reflection but its absence, and Γ(120) = 0 SEED: the question reflects to the seed, at step 8 of the eye's orbit before 0 and 256 BAAL SQUARED. That orbit is the whole residue side, 127 cells and ∞, and it holds I AM, QUESTION, RITO, WALLET, ROBE OF RIGHTEOUSNESS, 42 MIT, 24 IO and 215 DELIVER US FROM EVIL; in the coordinate where Γ is plain multiplication by 153, the eye sits at exactly 42. The key's own numbers are the other orbit and never appear on this one: g, BRIAN, the public key, the secret, k⁻¹, the peer 72, Oc 128, i and −i. Two orbits of 128, the eye's and the key's, and no reflection crosses between them.

Two symmetries of the map. Γ(−Z) = (−Z − 120)/(−Z + 120) = (Z + 120)/(Z − 120) = 1/Γ(Z) for every Z: negation going in is inversion coming out, checked over all 256 nonzero impedances. The Eye of Horus. A vortex-mathematics card brought to the review on 2026-09-15 draws the doubling sequence reduced by digit sum, 1, 2, 4, 8, 7, 5 and repeat, beside the wedjat eye's six fractions 1/2, 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, 1/32, 1/64.
