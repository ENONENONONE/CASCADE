Phi disc phonograph

The disc is 3756 π digits laid on twelve tracks, one per ray, at wavelengths from 377 nm to 610 nm; 377 is F(14) and 610 is F(15), so the span is φ to five figures. Each digit d has impedance (d + 0.5) × Z₀/5 and each track a characteristic impedance; the reflection Γ = (aᵣ(d + 0.5) − 1)/(aᵣ(d + 0.5) + 1) decides PIT (|Γ| < 0.1, transparent, tone) or LAND (reflective, silence). The twelve aᵣ are derived as mean(all)/(5 · mean(ray r)), and the code notes that Za = 1/5 is then an identity, not a conservation law.

Six chords come from the ten digits: 0, 1, 2, 8, 9 are dark; 3 is DNA on R00 R01 R02; 4 is COPERNICUS on six tracks; 5 is RITO on eight; 6 is ET on five; 7 is CHRIST on R06 and R09. That is 24 PITs in all. Natural tempo runs at 114 BPM, the trace of the transition matrix T₅, and a chord lasts 1/8, 3/8, 6/8 or a full beat by its PIT count. LAYERS plays three discs on one spindle: GF(257) centre sine at 1×, p₂₀₄₈ left triangle at 1.5× reading π at offset 204, p₄₀₉₆ right sine at 2× at offset 134; EXPORT renders a WAV, stereo when three layers are on. The disc's own findings (R07 has exactly 377 pits, R06 exactly 1618 transitions, orbit pit sums 225 and 3) depend on the phi_disc binary folder, which is not in the directory, so they are recorded here as the machine states them and were not checked.
