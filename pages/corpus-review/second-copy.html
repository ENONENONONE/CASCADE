The second copy, and the editions

The directory reviewed here is not the only copy of the corpus on the machine. cascade_machine holds a working copy, under version control, of the same files plus firmware, disc audio scripts and a Rust workspace, and its machine was edited at 15:41 on 2026-09-04, after this review was built. That copy of v4 is 28,132 lines against the directory's 27,586, and it already carries corrections that this review only reports: the AUTH text prints dlog(83) = 15, the GEN preset's digits encode S161, OBSERVE calls the observer step 16, and three commands are new, ILEDGER for i-value coverage, IFIELD for the extension-component rotation, CURIE for the iron and cobalt structure, with a note that the figure count decides a position rather than the point. Its embedded dictionary has 555 words: the JSON's 554 plus COBALT CURIE at 103, the 1388 K placement, and HAGGAI at 148, which reads like the sentence "i148 sits at 33 HAGGAI" transcribed as a word at 148; HAGGAI is the letter-sum word at 33 and at 148 it has no derivation. Neither word is in the JSON tablet, so the two copies now disagree at 103 and 148.

The editions on 2026-09-10. The second copy's machine was edited again at 11:13 today, 29,558 lines now, and its embedded dictionary is the largest edition by a distance: 967 words on the 257 positions, 413 of them not in this directory's JSON, and one word the other way, COBALT CURIE at 118, which the machine places at 103 instead. Of the 413, 410 sit at their letter sums and three are value-placed, COBALT CURIE at 103 from 1388 K, HAGGAI at 148 and SATOSHI at 181 beside SATOSHI NAKAMOTO. The additions are densest low in the field, 66 below 32 and 108 between 32 and 63, and 73 of them have three letters or fewer, ADA and CAB at 6, DC and DAB at 7, ADD at 9, so the letter-sum layer now covers the small positions several times over and 27 carries ten words. Higher up they are names and phrases: THIRTEEN at 99, ORDER AND CHAOS at 125, LIGHTNING NETWORK at 206, SCHNORR SIGNATURE at 209, DELEGATED PROOF OF STAKE at 210, RIEMANN HYPOTHESIS at 218. Words that earlier passages of this review had to mark as another edition's are this edition's. In all, the machine's 967 words are 846 at letter sum and 121 value-placed. The Curie placements are two sources. This directory reads cobalt 1403 K → 118 and nickel 631 K → 117; START reads cobalt 1388 K → 103 and nickel 650 K → 136, the solid-state page's figures from register item 12; the machine takes cobalt from START and nickel from here. RHENIUM Z is in this directory and the machine and not in START. So three editions of 555, 555 and 967 words disagreed at the two Curie points, at 75 and at 148. Later the same day the 413 words were merged into this directory's JSON: every word the machine has that the JSON lacked was appended to its entry in the machine's order, 205 entries touched, no other byte of the file changed, the rendered HTML given the same words, and the Rust crate's count updated. This directory's tablet now has 1119 word entries, 1112 distinct, 996 at their letter sums and 123 value-placed, and is a superset of the machine's dictionary. Two words now stand at two positions each, COBALT CURIE at 103 and 118 from the two Curie sources, and HAGGAI at 33 by its letters and at 148 as the machine placed it; the tablet already carried IDENTITY at 1 and 225, so a word at two positions has precedent. The START JSON is unchanged at 555 and still differs at its Curie placements. Every reading earlier in this review was made against the 555-word tablet, and the counts in the tablet section now say which.

The same copy contains cascade_rust, a fourteen-crate workspace from 2026-08-23 to 08-25 with core, vm, crypto, data, io, mesh, render, audio, ui, serial, window and wasm crates, so the field and tablet crates built for this review overlap an existing cascade_core and cascade_data. Reconciling them is a decision, not a finding.

A third edition of the dictionary was found on 2026-09-04 in the one place the directory cannot show: the bwave firmware on an ESP32-C6, read over its serial port. It has BISMUTH at 83, HAMSA at 5, HAFNIUM at 173, YTTERBIUM at 154, HOLY OF HOLIES at 251 and PRAYING MANTIS at 103, agrees with the tablet at 4 and 252, and carries the correct digits of π where the machine's p column does not. The readout is in the section on the other side of the serial.

Searching for the older edition that has APOSTLES at 12 and LUNAR at 13 found it nowhere on the machine: not in this directory, not under START, not in the second copy or its history, and not in any of the four older machine editions under START. Every edition found has 7 = G, CD, BAD; 12 = L, KA; 13 = M, AGE; and 15 = O, FED, AN, with IRON MELTING, IRON CURIE and the atomic-number entries arriving from v3 on. So five word lists exist over one unchanging set of derived fields, the older edition would be a sixth, and 15 = O is the one fact about those positions that holds in all of them. Under the letter-sum rule LUNAR is 66 and COVEN is 59, so if they are added they would be value-placed count-words, and a coven is thirteen by tradition rather than seven.
