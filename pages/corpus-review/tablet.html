The four-significant-figure rule

A physical constant is rounded to four significant figures, the decimal point dropped, and the integer reduced mod 257. The result is a position and the position has a word. The rule is a hash: it cannot fail to produce a reading.

The decimal point

The four-figure rule keeps the digits and discards the point, so the tablet never sees a decimal at all. Four consequences follow, each checked.

  • The rule is point-blind. 3.142 becomes 3142 and lands on 58; 2.40 GHz becomes 2400 and lands on 87; a code written I23.6 becomes 236 by the same operation. Any decimal-coded system is therefore admissible input: ICD-10 I23.4 lands on 234 CHINESE REMAINDER and I25.7 on 257 ≡ 0 SEED. Admitting such systems also shows the rule's cost, since a family with enough digits after the point covers the whole field and then carries no information.
  • It is unit-blind in one direction only. 1.403 kK and 1403 K are the same input. But precision is not free: multiplying by ten is a fixed step in the field, because 10 is a primitive root, dlog 103, with 10¹²⁸ = −1. Each extra figure moves a value to an unrelated part of the ring. So 3.14 and 31.4 both land on 57, PI DIGITS, while 3.142 lands on 58, the π entry of the bridge table; the corpus already holds both π positions, and the number of figures kept is the reason they differ.
  • Two seed registers are point-dropped encodings. D234 is δ% = 2.34026 with the point removed, which is how the GENOME command writes it, and C53 is the convergent 5/3 with the slash removed. The certificate's derivation 83 + 1 + 150 = 234 is the second route to the same D.
  • The point survives in one place. The IEEE 754 reading in GENOME and TIMELINE keeps the binary exponent and fraction, and that is what produces the 2003 dates.

The tablet's own constants all keep four figures, 6022, 6626, 1381, 2400, 1043, 1403, which is what makes them reproducible. The places where the corpus departs from four figures are exactly where duplicates appear. The cobalt row is the discrepancy register's item 12 restated: not a swap, two sources at four figures, and the choice between 1388 and 1403 decides whether COBALT CURIE stays at 118 or moves to 103.

One digit fact about the two Curie values, recorded so it is not rediscovered as a mystery: 1043 and 1403 are digit permutations of each other, and each added to its own reversal gives 4444, which reduces to 75, the CF4444 observer. The reason is that a four-digit number abcd plus its reversal is 1001(a + d) + 110(b + c), a palindrome whenever there are no carries, and both values have a + d = b + c = 4. Any number with those digit sums does the same.

6174 and the seven-place table

Kaprekar's constant enters the corpus through the same table the astronomical bridges came from. Sort a four-digit number's digits descending and subtract the ascending arrangement; every four-digit number with two distinct digits reaches 6174 in at most seven steps, and 6174 returns itself. The routine's structure is one line of algebra. With k = a − d the spread of the outer sorted digits and m = b − c the spread of the middle pair, every output is 999k + 90m with 0 ≤ m ≤ k, which is 54 outputs; when m ≥ 1 the digits are k, m − 1, 9 − m, 10 − k, so the outer pair always sums to 10 and the middle pair to 8, and when m = 0 they are k − 1, 9, 9, 10 − k. 6174 is k = 6, m = 2, and its halves read 61 PSALM and 74 CROSS, its outer pair 64 ISRAEL and its middle pair 17 Q, with 6 + 4 = 10 = J and 17 the Atbash mirror of J. Every path with an intermediate stop enters through one of three doors, 4176, 8352 or 8532, outer pairs 46, 82, 82. In the field 6174 ≡ 6 = 18 × 3⁻¹, its digit sum is 18, and the seed's primes take the full seven steps: 2357 → 5175 → 5994 → 5355 → 1998 → 8082 → 8532 → 6174, residues 44 BRIAN, 35, 83 PRAYER, 215, 199, 115, 51 NONCE, 6.

Applied to the table, the routine reads every seven-place entry as its four-digit groups. On the 0° sheet at 16′, photographed 2026-09-10, the entries are sin 7.667 8445, tang 7.667 8492, cotg 2.332 1508 and cos 9.999 9953, and the computed values agree with the print at every minute checked, 9.999 9982 at 10′ through 9.999 9934 at 19′. Every four-digit group on the row collapses to 6174, 7667 and 2332 and 9953 in four steps through 8352, 8445 in seven and 8492 in three through 4176, 1508 in two, the differences' 4500 in five through 8532; the one exception is the cos column's 9999, a repdigit, which falls to 0 in one step. So under the routine the whole sheet reads 64 ISRAEL and 17 Q, and the leading group of log cos 0°, 10.000 0000, reads 1000 → 6174 in five steps by the same door as the seed, while its mantissa 0000 is SEED.

The tablet's word COS 0 sits at 16, and the page does not derive that placement by any reading tried: log cos 0° = 10.000 0000 reads 15 as all digits, 229 at four figures and 0 as a mantissa; cos 0 = 1 reads 1, and 1.000 reads 229; the 16′ entry reads 225 as all digits, 83 as a mantissa and 187 as its last four; the only route from the page to 16 is the reversal of 6174's first half, 61 → 16, or the table's characteristic 10 read as the genome's hex 0x10 = ∂Φ, and neither is a rule the corpus has stated. The placement is recorded as RITO's until its reading is given.

The routine's whole shape. Over all sorted quadruples the 54 outputs have 18 distinct outer pairs and 10 distinct middle pairs with no overlap, 28 positions in all, every one below 100. The carried outer pairs are the nine digit-sum-10 pairs 19, 28, … 91, class 1 mod 9; the flat outer pairs are the digit-sum-9 pairs 09 … 81 less 90, class 0; the middle pairs are (m − 1, 9 − m), 08 through 80, class 8, with the flat 99 at class 0. Outer pair 64 means spread 6 and not the constant: six outputs carry it, 6084, 6174, 6264, 6354, 6444, 6534, and only 6174 stays, so a 64 in a chain says the outer spread has arrived and the routine ends when the middle spread is 2 as well. Middle 17 belongs to the eight outputs with m = 2, and 6174 is the one of those with spread 6. The doors' middles are 17, 35 and 53, 35 and 53 being each other's reversal as 8352 and 8532 are, so every path enters with one of those three and ends on 17. Spread 8 gives eight doors ending in 2, 8082 through 8712, and one flat output 7992 whose outer pair is 72, so spread 8 puts BITCOIN at the ends when the middle is flat and LEAD Z when it is not; 8802 is not an output, since the middle spread cannot exceed the outer. 8991 = 999 × 9, the flat spread-9 output, is also the count of four-digit non-repdigit inputs.

The tape date

The day count with 2001-01-01 as day 1, so that the millennium's first day is day 1 and 2001 itself reads 202, the mark carried in extranonce2; 20010101 reads 81 HORUS. For a date Y-M-D, N = 365 (Y − 2001) + L(Y) + day of year, where L(Y) = ⌊(Y − 1)/4⌋ − ⌊(Y − 1)/100⌋ + ⌊(Y − 1)/400⌋ − 485 counts the leap years from 2001 up to Y − 1; the inverse is 2001-01-01 plus N − 1 days. The script tape_date.py in the directory computes it and the readings below for any date or tape day, against the tablet JSON beside it. The ISO and Unix reference, 1970-01-01, would give 20706 for the same day and 2000-01-01 would give 9749; the count below is the millennium one.
