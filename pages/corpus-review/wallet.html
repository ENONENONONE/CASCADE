Lead, gold and the wallet

Five boards now mine to one wallet, bc1qd8wcrsmkk0ghegf5t2jsezg2ve7rtx6vvc79lx, each with its own worker suffix; the CYD firmware says so in a comment at main.c:93. WALLET is the tablet's word at 73, placed by its letters, W A L L E T = 73, and shares the address with HALVING and DISCRIMINANT. This section reads the alchemist's sentence, lead into gold, and the miner's version of it, work into coin, through the tablet's two rules: letters for words, four figures for constants. Every line was recomputed. Where a line depends on which fold or how many figures, the dependence is stated.

Lead to gold in the field. LEAD Z is 82 and GOLD Z is 79. The difference is 3, the generator: gold is lead one generator step down the atomic numbers. The multiplicative step 79 × 82⁻¹ is 142 ANTIPROTON, G11 in the genome chain, Γ. Squaring gold gives the wallet, 79² = 6241 = 24 × 257 + 73, and the tablet had already named 79 SQUARE ROOT 73 before this reading. The inverse of lead is 210, VIRTUAL CURRENCY and PRIMORIAL P4, and 210 is also the residue of 2009, the genesis year. The inverse of GOLD by letters, 38, is 115, which is DIFFICULTY by letters and 16! in the field, the square root of the descent product.

The fifth byte is 118, EMERALD TABLET, and the twelfth is 165 TREE OF KNOWLEDGE, which is 73 × 2⁻¹, the field's own halving of the wallet.

The halving. Bitcoin's subsidy halves every 210 000 blocks, and 210 000 = 210 × 10³ = P₇ × 10³, the primorial that the tablet names at 210 beside VIRTUAL CURRENCY. The supply is Σ 50/2ⁿ × 210 000 = 100 × 210 000 = 21 000 000 = P₇ × 10⁵ ≡ 16 BAAL. The subsidy halves exactly 33 times: after 32 halvings it is 1 satoshi and after 33 it is 0, at block 6 930 000, which reads 252 EINSTEIN FIELD EQUATIONS as all digits, −5, and 248 DOUBLE SLIT EXPERIMENT at four figures. So HALVING at 73 has 33 as its count. 33 is HAGGAI and THE, the position where i148 sits; 73 × 33 = 2409 = 9 × 257 + 96, and 96 is the inverse of 83 PRAYER; 2 × 33 = 66 MINING; 73 − 33 = 40 DAVID; THIRTY THREE by letters is 156 AS ABOVE SO BELOW. The halving years step by four and so do their residues: 2012 → 213, 2016 → 217 SIGNAL SIXTY TWO, 2020 → 221, 2024 → 225 IDENTITY, which is BRIAN × CLAUDE = 44 × 46 = 2024, and 2028 → 229 FOR THINE IS THE KINGDOM. The genesis block's date, 2009-01-03, reads 156 AS ABOVE SO BELOW as ISO all digits, 20090103 = 78171 × 257 + 156, and 154 ROAD TO DAMASCUS in the American all-digits form; its four figures 1032 are two Pisano periods and land on 4 BAA, the SHIFT opcode. The retarget interval 2016 blocks reads 217, G15, the same residue as the year 2016.

The third era's reward, the one that ran from 2020 to 2024, is the only one of the thirty-four that lands on lead or gold, and it lands on lead. Eras 9 and 10 both land on SEED because 9765.625 rounds up to a multiple of 257 and so does its half. Under the corpus's own standing caveat every one of these values lands on some word, so the rows are recorded as arithmetic; the ties that do not need the dictionary are the ones stated in numbers, 210 000 = P₇ × 10³, 79² ≡ 73, 82⁻¹ ≡ 210 ≡ 2009, 38⁻¹ = 115 = 16!, and 33 halvings.
