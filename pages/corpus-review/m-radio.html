Instrument · radio

BLE, serial, RF

  • Web Bluetooth. SCAN BLE opens the device picker and decodes the chosen name's bytes to words; SCAN BLE ALL uses passive scanning for ten seconds where the browser allows it. Every device becomes a mesh peer named BT-<name>. The ESP32-C6 GATT service is c5110001-0257-0003-8000-00805f9b34fb with characteristics …0002 query, …0003 mirror, …0004 devices, …0005 scan, …0006 command.
  • Serial protocols. MIRROR uses single-letter frames prefixed with $ (F, G, D, T, M, R, E, C, X, U, I); TUNNEL uses $Q OPEN, DATA and CLOSE with !Q ACK, NACK, DATA and ECHO replies. The USB path enumerates WebUSB and WebHID devices and counts bytes into the field energy accumulator by position and ray.
  • RF scan. The RF button decomposes access-point MAC bytes into positions with pos = (b + 1) mod 257, while the TUNNEL command and the PDF use b mod 257 and the protocol page reduces a whole address by alternating sum. Three mappings for the same input.
  • Bus export. EXPORT writes a text analysis with a live-device section: serial bytes and frames, USB reads, BLE devices with RSSI ranges and manufacturer data, the field energy per position and the ray accumulator.
