The seed quadratics

A pair with sum s and product p is the root pair of x² − sx + p, and that quadratic splits in the base field exactly when s² − 4p is a square mod 257. This is the same test that places φ in the fold, and applied to the three seed variants it settles a question the lineage raises: whether the old E73S56 was something the base field could not solve. It was not. All three E, S pairs are base-field root pairs; only φ's quadratic fails the test.

The KINGDOM quadratic

One quadratic in the same family is written down by the tablet's own letter sums. FOR THINE IS THE KINGDOM sits at 229 by letters, and its pieces are FOR 39, THINE 56, IS 28, THE 33, KINGDOM 73. The first four sum to 156, which is 229⁻¹, since 229 × 156 = 35724 = 139 × 257 + 1, and the last is 73. So the phrase says 229 = 229⁻¹ + 73, and an element equal to its own inverse plus 73 is a root of x² − 73x − 1:

x − 1/x = 73 ⟹ x² − 73x − 1 ≡ 0 (mod 257) roots 229 FOR THINE IS THE KINGDOM and 101 AMPLITUDE 229 + 101 = 330 ≡ 73 229 × 101 = 23129 = 90 × 257 − 1 ≡ −1 discriminant 73² + 4 = 5333 ≡ 193 = −64 = (8i)² = 128² roots (73 ± 128) · 2⁻¹ = (73 ± 128) × 129

It has φ's shape, x² − sx − 1 with roots x and −1/x, with KINGDOM in place of 1, and 101 = −1/229 stands to 229 as ψ stands to φ. Where it differs is the discriminant: 193 is a square, its root is 128, the fold point, so 229 = (KINGDOM + TRANSFERENCE)/2 and 101 = (KINGDOM − TRANSFERENCE)/2 and the equation splits in the base, while φ's discriminant 5 does not and φ needs the fold. Across the whole family x² − sx − 1, 128 values of s give a non-square discriminant and put the roots in the fold as φ's does, 126 split in the base as KINGDOM's does, and two have a double root: s = 32 gives (x − 16)² and s = 225 gives (x + 16)², because s = ±2i makes the discriminant 32² + 4 = 1028 = 4 × 257 ≡ 0. The hinge number of the π section is the discriminant that vanishes, and the double roots are i and −i.

The number 73 carries more than its place in this equation. It is WALLET and HALVING by letters, the E of the old seed E73S56, and 3⁴⁴, and its third word in the tablet is DISCRIMINANT, value-placed, so the tablet had tied 73 to a discriminant before this quadratic put it in the linear term. Read as an angle, tan 73° = 3.2709, which is the genome's ω₀/2α ≈ 3.27; the machine's beam quality Q = 10/(2 × 1.53) = 3.268, derived from the Airy width J₁(x)/x, has arctangent 72.986°, a gap of 0.014° from 73. The optical constant owes nothing to letter sums and the coefficient 73 owes nothing to optics, and no derivation joins the two ends, so the match is recorded at 0.014° as a coincidence, and waits. One loop closes through the tangent, and it is exact: the five pieces of the phrase written one after another, 39 56 28 33 73, make the integer 3956283373, and that integer and 3271, the four figures of tan 73°, differ by a multiple of 257, so both read 187 WAY TRUTH AND LIFE. The cosine and sine of 73° read 2924 → 97 POSEIDON and 9563 → 54 LOVE at four figures; every four-figure value lands on some tablet entry, so those two landings are not evidence of anything, whereas the agreement at 187 is a one-in-257 event between two independently built numbers. The structural angular facts belong to its neighbours: 72° is the pentagon angle with cos 72° = 1/(2φ), 73 = 72 + 1 is the observer's step past it, 72 is BITCOIN and 73 WALLET, and 5 × 73 = 365 closes a year in five arcs.

Square descent

D(n²) = (n − 1)²: 256, 225, 196, 169, 144, 121, 100, 81, 64, 49, 36, 25, 16, 9, 4, 1, 0. Seventeen positions, sixteen steps. Upper nine sum to 1356 ≡ 71, lower eight sum to 140 = T, all seventeen sum to 1496 ≡ 211. The product of the nonzero terms is 118, and the running product reaches 8 after five terms. All positions are quadratic residues on rays 0, 2, 4, 8, 10.

The nonzero steps are the squares 16² down to 1², so their product is (16!)². In the field 16! ≡ 115 and 115² ≡ 118, so the product is 118 with square roots 115 and 142; the running product reaches 118 at the fifteenth term and the closing ×1 leaves it there. Position 118 is EMERALD TABLET, the tablet's own name, with COBALT CURIE as its second word in this directory's copy; dlog 166, ray 10, inverse 159 SPEED OF LIGHT, negation 139 ENCRYPTION, chart fold 138 GLORIFICATION. EMERALD TABLET also sits at its letter sum: EMERALD is 58, where π lands at four figures, TABLET is 60 HOLY, and 58 + 60 = 118.

118 is the atomic number of oganesson, the element that closes period 7. The staircase's squares are the shell numbers of the periodic table: a shell with principal number n holds 2n² electrons, and the noble-gas closures 2, 10, 18, 36, 54, 86, 118 rise by 2, 8, 8, 18, 18, 32, 32, each 2n² used twice after the first, which is the Madelung order and the reason the closures are not the plain running sum of 2n². Read as positions the seven closures are B, J, CHAI, CASCADE, LOVE, TIKKUN and EMERALD TABLET; 36 is the krypton shell and the corpus's own name. Three chemistries meet at 118: the founding text of alchemy, whose stone is beryl coloured by chromium; the Curie point of element 27; and the last element of the table. COBALT CURIE and OGANESSON both sum to 109 by letters. The tablet's Z convention would put OGANESSON Z at 118; it has not been added.
