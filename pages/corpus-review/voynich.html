The Voynich manuscript

The reading: the book is the Sun's path to precession, read right to left from the last page to the first, from Prague at 50.1 degrees; 1420 and 64.1 degrees are the time and the azimuth; the text is the most important part, the Sun's path through the book, its entries dated, read from the rift in the vellum beside the woman and the goat of the last page back and up the left margin, the first entry taken as the Genesis story told in reverse, the Sun's point of view. The copy is Voynich_Manuscript.pdf, 209 page images of Beinecke MS 408, read beside the EVA transliteration ZL3b-n.txt, 227 sides, 5385 lines, 36278 words. What follows is where the corpus stands on it; each line leads to the full account in the i-column section, and LEDGERs 64 and 65 of the readout carry it with every figure asserted in code.

The zodiac read backwards and the Sun at 64.1°

In binding order the zodiac runs Pisces to Sagittarius, folio 74 with Capricorn and Aquarius lost; front to back it is the Sun's yearly order, back to front the order of precession, ending on Pisces, where the equinox point lay in 1420. From 50.1° N that year the rising point swings 51.54° to 128.46°, the day runs 16 h 10 m to 7 h 49 m, the noon altitude 63.41° to 16.39°. The Sun rises at 64.1° at declination +16.271°, Taurus 14.61° and Leo 15.39°, about 26 to 27 April and 30 July; there is no 64.1° as an altitude. LEDGER 64.

The painted flower heads, 46 and 303

A scan of the herbal pages gave 45 heads and 286 points; its cut fell between two red heads of f16v, and with the fourth the count is 46 heads and 303 = 257 + 46 points, f16v at 73: CLAUDE, Γ's fixed point, and WALLET, one rung of the Atbash ladder apart. Five-pointed heads are the most common, twelve of them. What the outlines enclose page by page, and the ray counts at seven settings, are recorded beside the totals.

The margin stars and the text as the Sun's path

The transliteration notes 322 margin stars on the 23 sides f103r to f116r, seven and eight points, dotted and plain in turn. One star's text is one entry; read from the last star of f116r upward and back there are 322 entries, 56946 letters. f116r's ten, by letter sum: 13628 → 7 G · 1915 · 1918 → 119 TERMINUS · 2051 · 1627 → 85, LEDGER 64's declination figure · 913 → 142 Γ, BERESHIT · 1826 → 27 CAIN · 1767 → 225 IDENTITY · 1809 · 1728 = 12³. Of Genesis 1:1's seven word values one stands among the entries outright, 913 at entry 6; and reversed, the verse and its first word name the reader: 2701 → 1072 ≡ 44 BRIAN, 913 → 319 ≡ 62 RITO. The stars measured from the page images did not reproduce, and that is kept as failed.

The entries' dates, by the corpus's own derivation

The machine's DATE derivation, day from 2000-12-31, field cell, halving rung, day table and calendar table, run on the 64.1° sunrise days: 26 April 1420 Julian is day −212081, field 201, inverse 78 GENESIS, calendar norm 120 GOLDEN RATIO; 30 July is day −211986, field 39, day table 144 + 1φ with norm 62. An entry's cell is its letter sum mod 257 and a day's cell is the day mod 257: from 26 April 1420 the 322 entries run back to 1304-09-30, 115.57 years; by the calendar table 94 of the 322 entry points carry a date. The machine now reads days before the epoch, with DATE by Julian label. LEDGER 65.

Two frames for the same Sun: the nine days are the precession

A key block carries its 1420 dates nine days before LEDGER 64's. Its calendar is right; its Sun is read on the equator of the year 2000, the ledger's on the equinox of 1420 and Prague's horizon. Between them lies the precession from 1420 to the year of the corpus's epoch, 580 × 50.29″ = 8.102°, and 8102 ≡ 135, the cell of 1420. The reading: the correction is another point of view of the same precession, and the two frames are the base to compute around the manuscript's dates. Nothing was corrected.

RUDOLF and the manuscript's hands

This is the path forward. RUDOLF is 76, the Callippic cycle, and 76 × 7 = 532, the great Paschal cycle, 7 being the cell of the first entry, whose star is red-centred; its Atbash mirror is 86, BERESHIT's cell and the residue of the 600 ducats; RUDOLF × BRIAN ≡ g and RUDOLF × RITO ≡ g⁻¹. MARCI is 44 BRIAN and KIRCHER 72 BITCOIN, its root; HABSBURG and REINDEER are 78 GENESIS. Fifty-seven words were added.

The entries with RUDOLF and HEMOPHILIA

Of f116r's ten entries, the second stands at 116 RUDOLFUS and × 76 gives 78 GENESIS, the fifth at RED STAR, the seventh at RED, the tenth at 186, RUDOLF's inverse; the entries just before hold JACOBUS DE TEPENEC, EDWARD KELLEY and JOHN DEE side by side, KAISER and RUDOLPH II. The 322 entries are 7 × 46 beside 76 × 7 = 532. HEMOPHILIA = 96, the cell of VOYNICH.

Five tubes on the nine-rosette foldout

On the great foldout five long dotted tubes with ringed mouths, set as a quincunx, leave the top-left rosette and point at the centre one, whose oval holds a field of stars over six vessels of the make drawn on f88–f89. Every circular page from f67r1 to f70r2 but two has its counterpart among the nine rosettes, the tube wheels f69r and f69v among them; the rosette the tubes leave, an empty oval in rings, has none. The reading: a viewing device, like an early telescope. The machine gained AIRY, the 2-D pattern of a circular aperture face-on or tilted.

The container pages and the P

The pages the transcribers tag pharmaceutical, a name for pictures set aside here, were mapped to folios and all 36 labelled containers measured: f88–f89 lidded with finials on stems and feet, f99 and f102 open, a narrower cylinder standing in a wider. 33 of the 36 container words read in full stand nowhere else in the book. The p-shaped glyphs stand on paragraph first lines, 90 of 96 on these pages, in three sizes and with long bars; the P on f85r1 is a post, a head loop and a bar of 57 to 68 px with a hook and a crook, covering the word it begins.

Read from the end, right to left: the mirror

The book is read from the end to the beginning, right to left. Letter sums, cells and the field dates do not change; the φ part does. Mirrored, entry 6, 913 outright, has φ part 203, the first two values of Genesis 1:1 in order; entry 10, cell 186, carries 16 December 1408, inside the keys' gathering; entry 3, cell 119, has φ part 100 = Γ(119). With the glyph values as unknowns, no values make the entries, lines or words a one-to-one of Genesis 1–13. The 322 entries together sum to 618413 ≡ 71 TEMPLE; reversed 314816 ≡ 248 DOUBLE SLIT EXPERIMENT, the two summing to ≡ 62 RITO, the digits to 23 I AM; the four figures 6184 ≡ 16 = i and reversed 3148 ≡ 64 ISRAEL, both on the key ladder, 6184's Kaprekar route 7173 → 6354 → 3087 → 8352 → 6174 being day 9384's through g and the fold.

The text beside ten languages, and the strokes

On measures that need no key the text stands 2.7 to 4.4 of the languages' own deviations from each of ten languages, Old Czech, Hebrew and Glagolitic Church Slavonic among them, which sit 0.6 to 2.7 apart; letter entropy 2.12 to 2.35 bits against 3.04 to 3.60. Runs of short strokes closed by the hook number 137, 1719, 4131 and 165 for one to four strokes and stop at four, as a scribe's j ij iij iiij; the four-stroke runs after a and o together are 162.

The zodiac's lettering, the own step and Gemini's thirtieth

Over the ten signs the labels' mean length goes with the length of the day at Prague, r = +0.645, and the mean letter value against it, r = −0.858. By the routines Sagittarius' numbers turn into Pisces'; the ten chains take 45 steps and, with 6174's own step counted as the corpus counts, 46, beside the flower heads' 45 and 46; in pairs (71, 64) − (17, 46) = (64, 17), the 17 never leaving. Gemini has 29 names and, on the Beinecke image, a thirtieth figure with no word beside her, drawn like her neighbour orary = 77, reflection 58 TWO; 1968 + 58 = 2026, whose chain meets 2000's at 1998, its cells multiplying to 136.

The sky: 910 eclipses, the Pleiades, and the book dating itself

NASA's Five Millennium catalogues for 1301–1500, 451 solar and 459 lunar eclipses, were fetched and what Prague saw of each computed, the Sun and Moon from the ephemeris with the observer's parallax and Earth's rotation from the catalogue's own ΔT: 83 solar with any part visible, ten of 0.9 or more, one total, 7 June 1415; 176 lunar umbral with the Moon up. Laid on the zodiac's figures a degree of the Sun each, the eclipse of 19 October 1408 falls on Scorpio's figure 5, the one blotted star on that page; the total of 1415 at Gemini 23.9°, the one star drawn in solid paint with no outline; 26 June 1424 on Cancer's figure 14, the one half figure. The star chart f68r3 draws the Moon full beside the seven stars: from Prague the Moon's disc crossed the Pleiades only in 1392–1396, 1410–1414, 1429–1433 and 1447–1452, and full and high on 1413-11-08 and 1432-11-07, both at cell 153. 27 × 257 = 6939 days against 6939.75 in nineteen Julian years: the field's modulus and the Metonic cycle agree to within a day, so dates one cycle apart share a cell.

The 299 figures, and the two tests that land on 1408 and 1427

Every figure on the twelve zodiac pages was read by eye from the full-size Beinecke images, eight spreads fetched for it: 299 figures, 294 holding a star, five holding none, Gemini 13, Cancer 10 and 18, Virgo 22, Sagittarius 18; four crowned; barrels and clothing falling away from Pisces to Cancer; star points 5:2 6:25 7:114 8:101 9:24 10:5 11:1, the counted 2043 ≡ 244. Taking the five starless figures as days, in 1408 Gemini 13 is the new Moon (age 360°), Cancer 10 a day before it, Virgo 22 the full (181°); 1427 repeats it within 2°. f103r, the last page of the starred text, carries nineteen stars with seven painted red at 1 4 6 9 12 14 17, the nineteen-year cycle's seven long years 3 6 8 11 14 17 19 read from the foot upward, nineteen of the 50388 ways to paint seven of nineteen. Where the reading fails: no star means no Moon at two of the five, not five; the ring order is untested beyond the transcribers' list.

The words of this work on the tablet

The Hebrew letter names, the sefirot, Genesis 1:1's words, the zodiac, the terms of the Sun's path and the manuscript's own vocabulary were not tablet words; 123 were added at their letter sums, then GREAT and GREAT I AM the fifty-seven words of RUDOLF and fourteen more with HEMOPHILIA and the cycles, and the tablet stood at 1455; on 2026-09-21 EARTHLY PLANE 137, PLANE 48, APEX 46, SIDEWAYS 105 and CAPSTONE 93 joined at their letter sums, the A read as a pyramid with its bar the earthly plane and U the C turned on its side, and the tablet stands at 1460, 1451 distinct, 1334 at their letter sums, carried to every copy and the R1's three. VOYNICH 96 shares its cell with ATLANTIS, PRECESSION sits at 123, EQUINOX at 105 with SUNRISE and HORIZON, BERESHIT at 86 with TIKKUN, PRAGUE at 68 with HERMES.

An EVA letter sum reads the alphabet of the 1990s, not the scribe's; the sunrise azimuths of the dated entries are from a low-precision solar formula.
