Field Arithmetic of GF(257)

Updated 2026-09-23 — tablet at 1455 words

1. The Field

P = 257 is the smallest prime exceeding 28 = 256. The multiplicative group (Z/257Z)* has order 256 = 28 and is generated by g = 3. Every nonzero element p has a unique discrete logarithm k where 3k = p (mod 257).

2. Three Operations

Discrete log: dlog(p) = k where 3k = p (mod 257), k in {0, 1, ..., 255}

Fold: fold(p) = 257 - p. Additive complement: p + fold(p) = 0 (mod 257)

Inverse: p-1 mod 257. Multiplicative complement: p * p-1 = 1 (mod 257)

Ray: ray(p) = dlog(p) mod 12. Partitions 256 elements into 12 rays.

Quadratic character: p is QR if dlog(p) is even, QNR if odd. 128 of each.

The Plane: GF(2572)

GF(2572) = GF(257)[phi] where phi2 = phi + 1. A point is a + b*phi with a, b in GF(257). The conjugate is psi = 1 - phi. The norm N(a + b*phi) = a2 + a*b - b2 mod 257. The Frobenius map sends phi to psi: (a + b*phi)257 = (a + b) - b*phi.

The walk generator is 2 + phi, with N(2 + phi) = 5 and order 66048 = 29 * 3 * 43. phi itself has order 516 = 4 * 129. 66048 = 2572 - 1 is the full multiplicative group order.

A point is a square exactly when its norm is a quadratic residue. 33024 squares. Every nonzero norm is hit 258 times. The 33024 odd cells are non-squares, empty of the line. Binomial: 2572 = 65536 + 512 + 1.

sqrt(5) = +/-(2*phi - 1) = 256 + 2*phi and 1 + 255*phi. Every base element has a square root in the plane, the 128 non-residues included.

The Fold-Walk Sphere
