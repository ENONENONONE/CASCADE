Cascade Ring

GF(257)× = ⟨3⟩ as a 256-gon in exponent order · 1 at the top, −1 at the bottom, i left, −i right · 0 at the centre

The coset of a position at level 2ᵏ is every 2ᵏ-th vertex from it: a regular polygon with 256/2ᵏ corners. Its index is the low k bits of the dlog.

The same coset as an orbit under the level's multiplier: ×9, ×81, ×136 at levels 2, 4, 8, then ×2, ×4, ×16, ×256. A stride-three multiplier draws a star; −a stands at the halfway step.

A date read every way it has been read: American digits whole and at four figures, ISO, block-reversed, reversed, flipped in both conventions, and the year flipped. Each landing is pinned.

Positions are labelled by the emerald tablet's first word. Colour is the ray, dlog mod 12, tinted by the phi disc's track wavelength for that ray, 377 nm at ray 0 to 610 nm at ray 11. Everything shown is computed here from g = 3 and p = 257; the tablet words are the only stored data.
